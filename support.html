Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. There's no sign-up. Everything stays on your iPhone.

How do I move my notes to a new iPhone?
Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one, and the app's notes come with it. You can also export a PDF or text copy with "Take this with you".

Steps or sleep say "nothing noted", but I allowed Apple Health.
Check iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Quiet Chapter, and make sure each kind of data is switched on there and in the app (Settings › Data from your phone or watch).

A food's numbers look wrong.
Food lookups come from USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts, which is written by volunteers. Correct the numbers in the dish and the app remembers yours.

Gentle nudges aren't arriving.
Check that notifications are allowed for Quiet Chapter in iPhone Settings › Notifications, and that a Focus isn't holding them.

How do I erase everything?
In the app: Settings › Privacy and your data › Erase everything on this phone. It can't be undone.

The Quiet Chapter is a personal journal, not a medical device, and it doesn't give medical advice.
