The Quiet Chapter
Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

In short

Everything you note in The Quiet Chapter stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no server of ours, and no tracking.

  • We don't collect, see, sell or share your notes. We have no way to — they never reach us.
  • The only things that leave your phone are the words you type into The Tavern's food search, or a barcode number, sent to two public food databases to look the food up.
  • Apple Health data is only read, only for the kinds you switch on, and never leaves your phone.
  • You can lock the app, export everything, or erase everything at any time.

What the app keeps, and where

The app keeps what you choose to note, in a single file inside the app on your iPhone. That file is protected by iOS data protection, so it can't be read while your phone is locked.

It can include: food and drink, medicines and doses, sleep, mood and journal writing, symptoms, weight notes, movement, your own words and quick taps, nudge times, and your settings.

  • Your companion photo. If you choose a photo of your own, a small copy is saved inside the app. The original stays in your Photos library.
  • Backups. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the app's file is included, the same as other apps' data. Apple handles those backups under its own terms; we can't see them.
  • Face ID and passcode. If you turn on a lock, iOS checks your face or passcode. The app never sees or stores your face data or passcode.
  • Exports. A PDF or text file is made only when you tap "Take this with you", and goes only where you send it.

What leaves your phone

Only food lookups. When you search in The Tavern, scan a barcode, or tap "Look up online" on an ingredient, the app sends what you typed or the barcode number to two public food databases:

ServiceWhat is sentRun by
USDA FoodData CentralYour search wordsU.S. Department of Agriculture
Open Food FactsYour search words, or a barcode numberOpen Food Facts, a non-profit

Nothing about you goes with these requests: no name, account, notes, Health data or device identifier from us. Like any website, these services can see the internet address the request comes from, under their own privacy policies (USDA, Open Food Facts).

If you never use the food search or barcode scanner, nothing leaves your phone at all.

Apple Health

The app reads from Apple Health only if you turn it on, and only the kinds of data you choose, one switch each: steps, sleep (with stages), resting heart rate, water and weight.

  • It only reads. It never writes anything to Apple Health.
  • What it reads is shown on your phone, beside your own notes, and nowhere else. It is never sent to us or anyone, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold.
  • You can switch any kind off in the app (Settings › Data from your phone or watch), or remove the app's access entirely in iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

What the app doesn't do

  • No account or sign-up, and no servers of ours.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party code that collects data.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information with anyone.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

Your choices

  • Lock the app with Face ID or your passcode, and separately lock The Meadow's private entries (Settings › Privacy and your data). Both are off until you turn them on.
  • Keep entries private. Meadow entries marked private read "kept private" everywhere else, and stay out of exports unless you include them.
  • Take a copy. "Take this with you" exports any place, or everything, as a PDF or text file.
  • Change or remove any single entry at any time.
  • Erase everything from Settings › Privacy and your data. This deletes every entry, setting and your companion photo from the app. It can't be undone, and it doesn't touch Apple Health.
  • Delete the app. Removing it from your iPhone removes all its data. Copies may remain in iPhone backups you made earlier, under Apple's backup settings.

Because we never receive your data, we can't see, correct or delete it for you. Everything above is in your hands on your phone.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anyone's information — we don't collect personal information at all.

Not medical advice

The Quiet Chapter is a personal journal. It doesn't diagnose, predict or give medical advice. Talk to a doctor about anything that worries you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. If a change ever meant data leaving your phone in a new way, the app would ask you first.
